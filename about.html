Research Methodology & Verification Protocol

To preserve rigorous academic integrity for college evaluation, this repository enforces a strict Zero-Fabrication Policy. In environmental science and disaster management, inaccurate statistics or fabricated relief efforts lead to misinformation. Therefore:

1. Primary Source Attribution

Every casualty count, flood level, and financial damage figure is cited from government post-disaster audits, official gazettes, or verified meteorological agencies.

2. Authentic Photographic Media

No AI-generated or synthetic disaster imagery is permitted. All photos are sourced from public domain archives, Wikimedia Commons, or government defense press releases with full attribution and licensing details.

3. Explicit Uncertainty Disclosure

Where official data is ambiguous or still undergoing multi-year scientific appraisal, it is explicitly flagged with "Needs verification" or "Documented - In Review" rather than conjectured.

Directory of Trusted Primary Sources

The case studies across the 5 domains are curated by cross-referencing these premier national and international environmental institutions:

National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA)

Apex statutory body headed by the Prime Minister for disaster planning, standard operating procedures, and state hazard mitigation guidelines.

Visit ndma.gov.in

National Institute of Disaster Management (NIDM)

Premier institute for human resource development, specialized capacity building, and empirical case study documentation of Indian disasters.

Visit nidm.gov.in/CaseStudies.asp

National Disaster Response Force (NDRF)

Specialized multi-disciplinary force deployed for specialized response during threatening disasters, floods, collapses, and chemical emergencies.

Visit ndrf.gov.in

India Meteorological Department (IMD)

National meteorological agency responsible for weather forecasting, cyclone tracking, monsoon monitoring, and severe rainfall warning bulletins.

Visit mausam.imd.gov.in

Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)

Statutory organization under MoEFCC providing technical services for air and water pollution prevention, river quality monitoring, and emission limits.

Visit cpcb.nic.in

Forest Survey of India (FSI) & MoEFCC

Publishes the biennial India State of Forest Report (ISFR), forest fire alerts (SNPP-VIIRS), canopy change analyses, and afforestation surveys.

Visit fsi.nic.in

Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC)

United Nations body for assessing the science related to climate change, sea-level rises, glacial recession rates, and extreme meteorological trends.

Visit ipcc.ch
